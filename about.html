About AMR Global Consultants

Established in 2017, AMR Global Consultants is a multidisciplinary consultancy firm bringing together Chartered Accountants, Company Secretaries, Cost Accountants, ACCA professionals, MBA graduates and other financial, legal and management specialists. Our aim is simple: to be a one-stop partner for businesses on compliance and governance, so our clients can focus on growing their business with confidence.

The team

A team of Chartered Accountants, Company Secretaries, Cost Accountants, ACCA and MBA professionals, along with other financial, legal and management specialists.

Each professional remains individually bound by the code of ethics of their own institute.
