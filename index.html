How we work

Six steps, the same for every engagement. You always see the scope and the fee before work starts.

  1. 1

    First conversation

    A call, WhatsApp or office visit to understand your needs.

  2. 2

    Requirement review

    We study your documents and current compliance position.

  3. 3

    Proposal

    A clear scope of work, timelines and fee quote.

  4. 4

    Engagement

    You confirm, and share documents.

  5. 5

    Execution

    Our team does the work, with regular updates.

  6. 6

    Delivery & ongoing support

    You receive the deliverables, and we track future due dates for you.
