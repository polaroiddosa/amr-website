Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions. For your own case, message us and we will reply with the next step.

Which business structure is right for me: proprietorship, partnership, LLP or private limited company?

It depends on your plans for growth, funding, liability and compliance cost. We compare the options against your business and recommend the best fit before you register.

How long does it take to register a company or LLP?

Usually 7 to 15 working days, once all documents are ready and name approval is received. Timelines can vary with government processing.

What are the annual compliances for a private limited company or LLP?

These include annual returns and financial statements with the Registrar, income tax returns, board and general meetings, and event-based filings. We prepare a compliance calendar and track every due date for you.

Is GST registration mandatory for my business?

It is mandatory once turnover crosses the prescribed threshold, and in some cases (such as inter-state supply or e-commerce sales) regardless of turnover. We check your situation and register you if required.

What documents do I need to start working with you?

It depends on the service. Typically we need PAN, Aadhaar and address proof of the owners or directors, business address proof, and any existing registrations or past returns. We share a checklist at the start.

Do you offer monthly packages for accounting and compliance?

Yes. We offer retainer arrangements covering bookkeeping, GST, TDS and routine compliance, priced to your volume of work.

Can you help with income tax or GST notices?

Yes. We review the notice, prepare the reply with supporting documents, and represent you before the department where needed.

Can you prepare project reports for bank loans?

Yes. We prepare detailed project reports, CMA data and financial projections in the format banks and subsidy schemes require.

Do you work with NRIs and clients outside Kerala or India?

Yes. We handle NRI taxation, FEMA and RBI compliance, Indian set-ups for foreign clients and overseas company formation, and we work with clients remotely.

How are your fees decided?

Fees depend on the scope, volume and complexity of the work. After understanding your needs, we share a clear written quote before starting.
